# Zoomies

**[▶ Watch the trailer](public/zoomies-trailer.mp4)**

A one-tap endless runner. Everyone in the same 30-minute round runs the same course. You get 10 ranked runs per round, and only your best one counts.

## Who can play

Anyone, kids included. One button to jump and one swipe to slide, so it's easy to learn on a phone, tablet or computer. Play in the browser with no download and no account.

## How to play

- **Tap / Space**: jump. Hold for a full jump, tap for a short hop.
- **Swipe down / ↓**: slide under pigeons. In the air, it drops you straight down into a slide.
- **Esc / P**: pause. From the pause menu you can end the run and keep your score.
- **Bones**: +10 each, and every bone from a ranked run adds to your Bones total.
- **Blue ball (Zoomies)**: double points for 6 seconds.
- **Close call**: clear a hydrant, cone, trash can, bench, crates or skateboard by a hair for +25.
- **Practice**: a random course, unlimited runs. Practice runs aren't ranked and don't earn Bones.

The street gets busier the further you run: hydrants and cones first, then trash cans (120 m), benches (220 m), pigeons (260 m), stacked crates (400 m), oncoming skateboards (600 m), and two-move combos after 900 m. The dog keeps speeding up as you go.

## Rounds

- A new round starts every 30 minutes, with a new course that's the same for every player.
- You get **10 ranked runs** per round. Your best run sets your rank.
- A blue **ECHO** flag on the course marks where your best run this round ended. Get past it to beat yourself.
- After each run, your result shows a strip of 100 m blocks: **gold** if you grabbed every bone, **green** if you got through clean, **red** where you crashed. Tap **Copy result** to share it.

## Leaderboards

- **Bones**: total Bones collected in ranked runs. This is the board you see first.
- **All time**: best single run per player across every round.
- **This round**: best run per player in the current round. It resets every 30 minutes.

Pick a display name before your first ranked run. No account needed.
